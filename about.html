About Us

PlumbConnect helps homeowners organize plumbing problems into clearer requests before speaking with independent local providers. The platform is not a plumbing contractor and does not perform the work.

Independent provider requests Clear service categories Customer verification reminders Provider terms confirmed directly

Our Story

Built to make plumbing requests simpler and more honest

PlumbConnect was created for the moment when a homeowner sees a leak, clog, cold water issue, or fixture problem and does not know how to describe it clearly to a provider.

The platform helps turn scattered symptoms into a better request: service category, urgency, location context, photos, access notes, and questions to confirm before hiring an independent contractor.

Core Values

What we stand for as a plumbing request platform

Clear Platform Role

The site helps organize requests and connect customers with independent providers; it does not perform plumbing work.

Respect for Context

Requests focus on symptoms, property access, urgency, photos, and details providers need before a useful conversation.

Verify Before Hiring

Customers are reminded to confirm license, insurance, reviews, estimates, warranty terms, and scope directly.

Pricing Transparency

Planning ranges are educational only. Final pricing and service terms come from the independent provider.

Trusted Guidance

Peace of mind starts with the right questions

Written Scope Before Approval

Customers should confirm inspection notes, likely scope, pricing terms, exclusions, and who is responsible for permits or parts before authorizing work.

License, Insurance & Warranty Terms

Any warranty, workmanship promise, insurance status, and license requirement should be verified directly with the independent provider before hiring.

Right Choice

Why homeowners use PlumbConnect

Clear request categories for leaks, drains, heaters, sewer lines, and fixtures. Better provider conversations with symptoms, urgency, access notes, and photos prepared. Verification reminders for license, insurance, written estimates, scope, and warranty terms. Transparent aggregator role so customers know the site does not perform or guarantee contractor work.
