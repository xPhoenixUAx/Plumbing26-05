Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

PlumbConnect ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you visit this website or submit a plumbing service request through the platform.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not use the website.

1. Introduction

PlumbConnect is an aggregator and request-matching platform. The site helps homeowners, property managers, and businesses organize plumbing-related requests and connect with independent local service providers where available. PlumbConnect does not perform plumbing work and does not control the privacy practices of independent providers after information is shared with them for request evaluation.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out contact forms, request provider options, communicate with us, respond to surveys, or submit details about a service need. This may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact method.
  • Service location or general service area.
  • Property type, service category, urgency, and request notes.
  • Photos, project details, fixture information, or other information you choose to provide.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the website, certain information may be collected automatically, including IP address and approximate location data, browser type and version, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring website addresses, device information, and interaction data.

2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, web beacons, analytics tools, and similar tracking technologies to understand browsing activity, improve website performance, remember preferences, and help measure request activity. For more details, please see our Cookie Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for request intake, provider matching, communication, analytics, website improvement, legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, and business operations. Where legally required, marketing communications are sent only with consent or where otherwise permitted by applicable law.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties. We may share information with independent service providers so they can evaluate whether to contact you about your request. We may also share information with vendors that support website hosting, analytics, communications, security, form processing, or operational services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, in connection with a business transfer, or with your consent. Service providers and vendors are expected to use appropriate safeguards for information they process on our behalf.

5. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption where appropriate, secure systems, access controls, monitoring, and periodic review of security practices. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and no website can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, opt out of certain communications, object to certain processing, or request restriction of processing. To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe information from a child under 13 has been submitted, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external sites. Review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. By using the website, you understand that information may be processed in jurisdictions with different data protection laws.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, process requests, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, and support business operations. When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted, anonymized, or securely retained as required by law.
