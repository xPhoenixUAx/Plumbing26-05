1. Introduction

PlumbConnect uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, support request forms, improve performance, understand visitor behavior, prevent misuse, and measure campaign activity where applicable. PlumbConnect is an aggregator and request-matching platform, not a plumbing contractor.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to make websites function more efficiently, remember preferences, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies can include:

  • Session cookies: temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until deleted.
  • First-party cookies: cookies set by the website you are visiting.
  • Third-party cookies: cookies set by a domain other than the website you are visiting.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, accessibility, form operation, and preference storage. You cannot opt out of essential cookies through the website because they are required for basic operation.

  • Cookie consent: remembers cookie preferences where a consent tool is active.
  • Session management: maintains basic website functionality while browsing.
  • Security controls: helps protect forms and reduce misuse or automated abuse.

3.2 Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website by collecting aggregated or pseudonymous information. This helps improve page performance, content structure, usability, and request flow.

  • Google Analytics or similar analytics: may track website traffic, page views, device information, and user behavior patterns.
  • Performance monitoring: may identify technical issues, page speed problems, and optimization opportunities.

3.3 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies enable enhanced features and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose tools are embedded on the website.

  • Preference storage: may remember language, region, or display preferences.
  • Form data support: may temporarily support form interactions or prevent duplicate submissions.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies may be used to understand campaign performance, measure advertising effectiveness, and support relevant advertising where campaigns are active.

  • Advertising networks: may measure ad performance, referral activity, and user engagement.
  • Social media or embedded tools: may enable sharing, attribution, or tracking by third-party platforms.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies or similar technologies on your device. These may include analytics providers, advertising platforms, map embeds, communication tools, security tools, and social media platforms. Third parties may collect information about your online activities across different websites.

We do not control third-party cookies. You should review the privacy and cookie policies of those third parties for details about their practices and choices.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to accept or reject many types of cookies. Most browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify settings to decline or delete cookies.

5.1 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Popular browsers such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge provide settings to block, delete, or limit cookies.

5.2 Opt-Out Tools

You may also be able to opt out of certain third-party cookies through tools such as Google Analytics Opt-Out, the Digital Advertising Alliance, and the Network Advertising Initiative.

Note: blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience. Some features, including forms, preference storage, embedded maps, security checks, or request tools, may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Because there is no uniform standard for interpreting DNT signals, this website does not currently respond to DNT browser signals or mechanisms.
